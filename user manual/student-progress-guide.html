Student Progress

A short user guide for the Office, Teachers and Guardians
শিক্ষার্থীর অগ্রগতি — ব্যবহার নির্দেশিকা

Student progress uses class-test results to show which students are slipping, so the school can act early. When a student needs help, the Office sets an improvement action: a small daily task for the teacher, the guardian, or both. When the action ends, the student's next test shows whether it worked.

Who does what

Office / PrincipalWatches and decidesOpens the board, sees who is at risk, creates actions and checks that the daily tasks get ticked.
TeacherDoes the school partSees the task on the Today screen and ticks “Done today” or “Couldn't today” each day.
GuardianDoes the home partSees the task on the Today screen (the first screen of the app) for their child and ticks it every evening.

How one action works

1 · SpotThe board shows a student is at risk or falling in a subject.
→
2 · PlanThe Office creates an action: what the teacher and the guardian do each day, for how many school days.
→
3 · DoEvery school day, the teacher and the guardian tick their task in the app.
→
4 · CheckAfter the last day, the next class test is compared with the one before: Before 32% → After 48%.

Words you will see

WordWhat it means
At risk ঝুঁকিতেThe student's overall marks are going down and they failed their most recent class test. (At least 2 tests are needed.)
Falling নামছেIn one subject, each of the last three tests was lower than the one before, for example 44% → 38% → 32%.
▲ / ▼The last test in that subject was at least 1 mark-percent higher (▲) or lower (▼) than the one before.
Improvement action উন্নতির কাজA daily task for a fixed number of school days, for one student or a whole section, in one subject.
Not marked চিহ্নিত হয়নিA school day has passed and nobody ticked “Done” or “Couldn't” for it.
Everything is calculated from published class-test results. If a test has not been published, it does not count yet. An absent student's test counts as an absence, not as a zero.

If a teacher is on leave and you cover their period, their task for that day appears on your Today screen with Covering, today only. Your tick counts as yours (C on the action's strip).

  • Didn't get the questions (প্রশ্ন/কাজ পাইনি)
  • Other (অন্য কারণ)
Please tick every evening, even if you couldn't do it. You can only tick today's task. Pressed the wrong button? Tap Undo the same day. If you miss 3 days in a row, the class teacher will contact you.

The little squares show your past days: green = done, orange = couldn't, red = not ticked. No card means there is no task that day (for example on holidays).

When the task ends, the school compares your child's next class test with the one before. Thank you for helping!

Quick answers

QuestionAnswer
Why isn't a new test showing on the board?Only published class-test results count. Once the result is published, it appears the next time the board is opened.
Why is a weak student not marked “At risk”?They need at least 2 tests where they were present, a downward overall trend, and a failed last test. A weak student who is improving is not at risk.
What is the pass mark?Each class test has its own pass mark; the board uses that test's mark.
Can a teacher see the board?No. Teachers see only their own tasks on the Today screen. The board is for the Office and the Principal.
Can guardians see marks or other children?No. Guardians see only the daily task for their own child.
Can I change an action's text after saving?No. Stop it and create a new one. You can add days with Extend by 5 days.
Does a holiday added later move the days?No. The days are fixed when the action is saved. Add days with Extend by 5 days if needed.
How is “Before → After” worked out?Before is the last test in that subject before the start day. After is the first test after the last day. For a class action, both are the section's average.
Can a teacher tick yesterday?No. Only the Office or the Principal can mark a past day, and it shows as O.
The Student progress menu item shows numbers. What are they?Those are the Class Test counters (papers to review, overdue results), not progress numbers.

A good weekly routine for the Office

  1. Open Student progress. Bring No action yet to zero by creating actions for at-risk students.
  2. Check Actions behind and follow up with those teachers.
  3. Look at finished actions (Action done). If a student did not improve, try a different task.
  4. Answer the 11:00 notification each school day: remind teachers, or mark on their behalf if you know it was done.
