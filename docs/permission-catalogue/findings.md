# Findings — gaps in TODAY's gates

These are pre-existing gaps that the permission inventory (origin/dev, 2026-10-20) found in today's gates. **The permission-matrix plan did not cause them.** The catalogue's defaults reproduce today exactly (R4), gaps included. A row that inherits a gap carries `F-n` in its flags column in [`README.md`](README.md) and in the area files.

**Status key.** VERIFIED means the file:line was opened on origin/dev during this consolidation and the behaviour confirmed. UNVERIFIED means it is carried from the inventory without re-reading.

**Severity key.**
- **High:** a guardian or any login can read or change data it must not.
- **Med:** a staff member can act beyond their role or row.
- **Low:** a latent risk, a mismatch, or a functional inconsistency.
