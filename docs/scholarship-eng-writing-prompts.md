# Class 5 English — the writing-prompt bank and its ledger

**Owner-supplied, 2026-09-15. This is BOTH the bank and the record of what has been used.**

Questions 13 (letter · application · email) and 14 (composition) are rubric-marked writing
tasks, and the owner keeps a fixed bank of prompts for them. The rule is:

> **Take the next UNUSED prompt of each kind, in listed order. Never repeat one.**

So it is a ledger, not a list. When you generate a paper, tick the rows you used **in the same
commit as the paper** and name the paper in the Used-by column. A bank with no ledger is a bank
that gets repeated, because "which ones have I used" is exactly the fact no one remembers and no
session inherits.

Spelling and capitalisation are normalised here (the source message had a few typos — *unhealty*,
*sportes*, *abour*, *premission*, *priece*). The wording is otherwise the owner's.

Related: `docs/scholarship-eng-paper-structure.md` (which question takes which kind),
`server/scripts/seed-scholarship-topics.ts` (the topic catalogue).

| Kind | Prompts | Used per paper | Papers covered |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Letter (13.a) | 13 | 1 | 13 |
| Application (13.b) | 5 | 1 | **5** |
| Email (13.c) | 5 | 1 | **5** |
| Composition (14.a + 14.b) | 9 | 2 | **4** |

**The bank does not run out evenly.** A paper that prints every form consumes one letter, one
application, one email and two compositions, so **compositions run dry after 4 papers and
applications and emails after 5**, while letters last 13. Ask the owner for more of the short
kinds before generating a fifth paper, rather than silently reusing one.

If a paper prints only ONE form of question 13 (which is what a real sitting does), it consumes
only that kind — the counts above assume the generator writes all three forms for the teacher to
choose from.

## The one row already ticked, and why

`scholarship/C5_ENG_Model_Question_Unit01_2026_approved.md` was written **before** this bank
existed, so its question 13 and 14 prompts were invented rather than drawn from here. Four of the
five are not in the bank at all and cost nothing. The fifth is: its `13.b` asks for **an
application to the Head Teacher for a library card**, which is application prompt 1 word for word
in substance. It is ticked so the next paper does not print the same task again.

Its other writing prompts — a letter describing the neighbourhood library, an email about a
biography, and the compositions *Our School Library* and *A Visit to a Library* — are outside the
bank and leave every composition row free.
