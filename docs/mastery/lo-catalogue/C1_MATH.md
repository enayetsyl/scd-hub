## Reviewer notes
- **No SK-ANS for C1.** The book forbids answer-sentences in word problems ("বাংলা বাক্য লিখবে না … বাক্য না লেখার জন্য নম্বর কাটা যাবে না", সিদ্ধান্ত ১৭). The "উত্তর-বাক্য নেই" tag must be disabled for C1, or it will route false weaknesses.
- **SK-WORK is the strongest marking signal in C1.** An answer with no working scores zero, and the class gives no partial marks ("এই শ্রেণিতে আংশিক নম্বর নেই"). A zero on a QT-SOLVE/QT-WORD item can therefore mean either a wrong answer or missing working. The tag is what tells them apart.
- **The C1 marks tables have no "কোন অধ্যায় থেকে" column.** QT→LO mapping comes from each chapter's "পরীক্ষায় যা আসবে" line. QT-MCQ (10 marks half-yearly, 7 annual) has no chapter mapping and is linked to no LO. Owner: say whether MCQ items should be tagged per item.
- **Splits beyond the ';' rule (owner to confirm):** 03-01/03-02 (অঙ্কে vs কথায় লেখা, because the exam has two separate question types); 09-01/09-02 (চেনা vs আঁকা); 12-03/12-04 (হালি-ডজন is written work, but সপ্তাহ-মাস/ক্যালেন্ডার is class-only by the book's rule). The bracketed "[ভাগ: …]" marks text added to the verbatim wording.
- **Oral নামতা LOs (06-01, 11-01) are rubric items.** The written evidence goes to SK-TABLE-01 and 06-02. Ch 11 has a single oral LO, so the owner may want a written twin, or may let SK-TABLE-01 carry it.
- **Consolidation chapters (5, 13) each carry one generic LO.** These LOs would double-count evidence already tagged to ch 3–4 and the whole book. Suggest the owner merge them into their source LOs or mark them roll-up-only.
- Ch 1 and 2 have no exam question (stated in the book). Ch 9 has no written classwork (সিদ্ধান্ত ১৯: দৃশ্য-ছবি, মুখে মুখে), yet annual Q11 does test naming and drawing.
- QT-MONEY exists only in C1; C2 and C3 test money through QT-FILL/QT-SHORT/QT-WORD and QT-UNIT/QT-FIGURE.
- Book flags kept for awareness: printing errors in ch 3 "মাঝের সংখ্যাটি লিখি" (অমীমাংসিত ০৪); no English number-words section, although the branch paper has 10 marks for it (অমীমাংসিত ০৩).
