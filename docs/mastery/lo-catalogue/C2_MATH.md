## Reviewer notes
- **No SK-ANS for C2.** The book says word-problem answers are digits only: "উত্তর-বাক্যের জন্য নম্বর নেই" (সি ১৮). The question rules also say an answer-sentence "চাওয়া যাবে না". The "উত্তর-বাক্য নেই" tag must stay off for C2. The answer-sentence habit first starts in C3 (D-০৭).
- **The marking rules are thin.** §১ gives only the full-marks condition for word problems; nothing covers partial marks or missing working. SK-WORK-01 and the SK-COL / গুণের ক্রম tags are therefore *proposed*: they come from the teaching and question rules, not from a deduction rule. Owner: confirm, or set deductions.
- **Splitting at ';' (owner to confirm):** in ch 5 the fragment "উত্তর ১০০০ পর্যন্ত" is only a range limit, so it was folded into 05-02 instead of becoming its own LO. Ch 5 and 6 "পাশাপাশি / খাড়ায়" were split further, into 05-01/02 and 06-01/02, because they are different skills. Ch 2 was split into অঙ্কে vs কথায় লেখা (separate QTs), and ch 11 into ছড়ায় বলা (rubric) vs গুণফল লেখা (written). "[ভাগ: …]" marks added text.
- Ch 1 is revision. Its LOs have no direct question; only নামতা (Q1) is examined from ch 1. Odd/even and ordinals are examined in 3-digit form through ch 4. Suggest these be roll-up/diagnostic only. The same applies to ch 16 (one generic LO, which double-counts).
- QT→LO mapping uses the table's "অধ্যায়" column, narrowed by each chapter's "পরীক্ষায় যা আসবে". Annual QT-FILL and QT-SHORT cover ch 10 and 13–15 broadly, so those LOs carry them by chapter even where the teacher note is less specific (e.g. 10-04 বিনিময়, 10-05).
- Ch 3 "তুলনা (> <)" has no separate question in either exam. Ch 8 "ছবিতে আকৃতি গোনা" is not named in the exam rules either. Both are kept as written classwork LOs.
- The chapter-15 name in the teacher note ("মুদ্রা — টাকা চিনি, বিনিময় করি, হিসাব করি") is longer than in the contents table ("মুদ্রা"). The contents name is used here.
- Candidate strand (not created): গুণ লেখার ক্রম "দল কয়টি × প্রতি দলে কয়টি" runs through ch 9–12 and C3. It is tracked here only as a tag on 09-01.
